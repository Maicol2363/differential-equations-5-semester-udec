# Steps to Solve

* **Identify the fraction** and ensure the degree of the numerator is smaller than the degree of the denominator; if not, use long division first.
* ***Factor the denominator*** completely into linear factors like (x - a) or irreducible quadratic factors like (ax^2 + bx + c).
* **Set up the decomposition** by assigning a constant (like A, B) over each linear factor, or a linear expression (like Ax + B) over each quadratic factor.
* ***Multiply both sides*** by the original common denominator to clear the fractions and create a single equation without denominators.
* **Solve for the constants** by either picking "easy" values for x that make terms zero or by comparing the coefficients of like powers of x.
* ***Substitute the values*** of the constants back into your original setup to write the final simplified sum of fractions.
